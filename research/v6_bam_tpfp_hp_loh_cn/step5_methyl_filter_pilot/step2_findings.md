   NME_imbalance + Epipoly_Delta to be non-null (HP-conditional, ~26-40% coverage). 
   For most FP-rich cells, n_fit (rows surviving dropna) is 30-70% of the step3 
   reported n (e.g. chr8|Inner|other|cov_normal: step3 n=625 → n_fit=152). 
   Interpretation: signal is for the HP-stratifiable subset, not the entire FP-rich zone.
2. **Small TP counts in UNKNOWN cells**: 4 of the 11 cells have n_TP_fit ≤ 5 
   (auto|UNKNOWN|*). filter_signal=1.0 in these cells is essentially trivial 
   (all TPs already excluded by P(TP)<0.5 since the cells are 95-99% FP).
3. **In-sample evaluation**: predictions come from the LR fit on the same rows. 
   Step 3 must add held-out CV or independent test (cross-sample) to confirm.
4. **Decision-theoretic τ choice**: best_tau=0.5 is the minimum τ in the grid. 
   The signal is driven mainly by Model B's high in-cell AUC. Step 3 ΔF1 may differ 
   substantially once global counts (not within-cell) are considered.
