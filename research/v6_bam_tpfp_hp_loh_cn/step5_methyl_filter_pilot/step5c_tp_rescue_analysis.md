## Limitations

- lost_TP n = 21 — very small; Mann-Whitney has limited power.
- removed_FP n = 338 — substantially larger; asymmetric power may inflate false positives in MW tests despite BH-FDR.
- Rescue rule thresholds derived in-fold from the same data they are evaluated on — per-fold validation in Stage 6 attempts to mitigate but is not a true held-out test.
- 4 AGGREGATED cells represent ~1.1% of master rows (404/35,332). Generalization to whole-genome filter is not assured.
