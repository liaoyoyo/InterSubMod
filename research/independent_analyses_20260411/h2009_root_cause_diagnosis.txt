  5. FP in LOH zones: 66 (out of 86)

Root Cause Hypothesis:
  H2009 is a lung cancer line with the highest mutation burden (168K truth SNVs).
  This results in:
  a) More germline variants escaping PON (lower PON rate)
  b) Higher PASS count with more FP among them
  c) FP heavily concentrated in LOH regions
  d) Methylation features add noise in LOH-dominant FP environment
  e) Phase 1A methyl+context degradation: features trained on
     other samples' TP/FP distributions don't generalize to H2009's
     LOH-dominated FP landscape
