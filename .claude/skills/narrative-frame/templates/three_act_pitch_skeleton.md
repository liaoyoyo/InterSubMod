# 3-Act Pitch Skeleton (Duarte)

> Nancy Duarte《Resonate》(2010) — 變革推銷

```markdown
---
framework: 3-Act Pitch
when: <變革推銷 / 轉型 deck / product launch>
---

# <Title>

## Act 1: Beginning — What is（25%）

<Establish empathy with audience's current world>
<Common ground — what we all agree on>

## Act 2: Middle — Call to Adventure（50%）

### 反覆對比 (What is ↔ What could be)

| What is（現狀） | What could be（願景） |
|----------------|---------------------|
| <現狀 1> | <願景 1> |
| <現狀 2> | <願景 2> |
| <現狀 3 — 最痛> | <願景 3 — 最吸引> |

### Resistance + Resolution

- 受眾會擔心什麼？
- 你如何 address？

## Act 3: End — New Bliss（25%）

<Vision of the future state — 達成後的世界>

<Specific Call to Action — 1 個 ask>

---

範例（説服 Lab 從 LR 轉 chr8 zone）:

- Act 1: 我們 6 cycle 都用 LR + caller_af；都卡住
- Act 2:
  - What is: cross-sample LR 不一致 ↔ What could be: chr8 zone 物理基礎強
  - What is: caller_af direction-inconsistent ↔ What could be: boolean rule cross-sample stable
  - What is: 12 月沒突破 ↔ What could be: 1 cycle 即可驗證 chr8
  - Resistance: PI 不想 sunk cost ↔ Resolution: 設 tripwire
- Act 3: Cycle 5 結束時 ISM framework 完整 cross-sample → paper 主軸鎖定

---

Framework: 3-Act Pitch (Nancy Duarte《Resonate》2010)
```
