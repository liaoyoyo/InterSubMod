## §1 結論先行 / 商務報告

### SCQA / Pyramid Principle / MECE

- **作者**: Barbara Minto
- **書**: 《The Pyramid Principle: Logic in Writing and Thinking》(2020 updated; originally 1978)
- **ISBN**: 978-0273710516
- **URL**: [barbaraminto.com](https://www.barbaraminto.com/)
- **背景**: 1960s 在 McKinsey 發展，後成為諮詢業標準
- **引用**: 「思考由下而上 (findings → recommendations → 結論)；溝通由上而下 (結論先講)」
- **教材**: [ModelThinkers - Minto Pyramid & SCQA](https://modelthinkers.com/mental-model/minto-pyramid-scqa)

### BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front

- **源**: US Army Field Manual 6-22 "Army Leadership"
- **URL**: [US Army FM 6-22](https://armypubs.army.mil/ProductMaps/PubForm/Details.aspx?PUB_ID=1001127)
- **背景**: 軍事 brief 標準格式；後被 government / 企業採用
- **引用**: 「To save senior decision-makers' time, lead every communication with the conclusion」

### Inverted Pyramid

- **源**: Journalism late-1800s（從 telegraph 電報時代發展）
- **URL**: [NN/g — Inverted Pyramid: Writing for Comprehension](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/inverted-pyramid/)
- **學術**: Mitra et al. 2020《Evaluating the Inverted Pyramid Structure through Automatic 5W1H Extraction》(UW)
- **引用**: 「Most important content at top; readers can leave at any point and still understand」

## §3 短時口頭答辯

### PREP

- **源**: 公共演講教材（無單一發明者）
- **URL**: [Toastmasters International — PREP Method](https://www.toastmasters.org/)
- **引用**: 「Point → Reason → Example → Point — fits any 30-second answer」

### ABT — And-But-Therefore

- **作者**: Randy Olson
- **書**: 《Houston, We Have a Narrative》(2015, University of Chicago Press); 《Narrative Is Everything: The ABT Framework and Narrative Evolution》(2019)
- **ISBN (Houston)**: 978-0226270845
- **ISBN (Narrative Is Everything)**: 978-1072232575
- **URL**: [randyolsonproductions.com](http://randyolsonproductions.com/)
- **背景**: 海洋生物學家轉電影 / 敘事教練；ABT 對應三幕劇精煉版
- **引用**: 「Every scene has an and, but, therefore in it. The ABT works at every scale — sentence, paragraph, paper, talk」
- **教材**: [Association of Science Communicators — Science of Storytelling](https://www.associationofsciencecommunicators.org/2025/the-science-of-storytelling/)

### Elevator Pitch

- **源**: Silicon Valley VC culture 1980s
- **背景**: 30s ride 期間 pitch idea 給 VC
- **引用**: 「If you can't pitch it in 30 seconds, your idea is not clear enough」

### Rule of Three

- **源**: 古希臘修辭學（Cicero, Quintilian）→ Aristotle《Rhetoric》
- **背景**: 人類短期記憶 magic number 3
- **引用**: 「Veni, Vidi, Vici (Caesar)；Life, Liberty, Pursuit of Happiness — 三點記憶最強」

## §4 故事敘事

### Pixar Story Spine

- **作者**: Kenn Adams（improv community 1990s）
- **背景**: Bay Area Theatresports 後被 Pixar 採用為 4th rule of storytelling
- **URL**: [storyplanner.com — Pixar Story Spine](https://www.storyplanner.com/story/plan/pixar-once-upon-a-time-story-spine)
- **教材**: [Aerogramme Studio — The Story Spine: Pixar's 4th Rule](https://www.aerogrammestudio.com/2013/03/22/the-story-spine-pixars-4th-rule-of-storytelling/)
- **引用**: 「Once upon a time… Every day… One day… Because of that… Until finally…」

### 三幕劇

- **作者**: Syd Field
- **書**: 《Screenplay: The Foundations of Screenwriting》(1979, revised 2005)
- **ISBN**: 978-0440576471
- **背景**: Hollywood 編劇教材；後 widely adopted by speakers
- **引用**: 「Setup (Act 1, 25%) → Confrontation (Act 2, 50%) → Resolution (Act 3, 25%)」

### 英雄之旅 — Hero's Journey

- **作者**: Joseph Campbell
- **書**: 《The Hero with a Thousand Faces》(1949)
- **ISBN**: 978-1577315933
- **後續**: Christopher Vogler《The Writer's Journey: Mythic Structure for Writers》(1992)
- **ISBN (Vogler)**: 978-1932907360
- **背景**: 比較神話學 → 1970s George Lucas《Star Wars》套用 → 1990s Hollywood 標準
- **引用**: 「12 stages: Ordinary World → Call → Refusal → Mentor → Threshold → Tests → Ordeal → Reward → Return」

### Freytag's Pyramid

- **作者**: Gustav Freytag
- **書**: 《Die Technik des Dramas》(1863, German)
- **背景**: 古典戲劇結構分析；後成 narratology 標準
- **引用**: 「Exposition → Rising Action → Climax → Falling Action → Resolution」

### Duarte Sparkline / Persuasive Story Pattern

- **作者**: Nancy Duarte
- **書**: 《Resonate: Present Visual Stories that Transform Audiences》(2010); 《slide:ology》(2008)
- **ISBN (Resonate)**: 978-0470632017
- **ISBN (slide:ology)**: 978-0596522346
- **URL**: [duarte.com](https://www.duarte.com/)
- **背景**: 分析 MLK "I Have a Dream" + Steve Jobs iPhone launch 等經典演講共同 pattern
- **引用**: 「What is ↔ What could be — 反覆對比 + Call to Adventure + New Bliss」

### Golden Circle

- **作者**: Simon Sinek
- **書**: 《Start with Why: How Great Leaders Inspire Everyone to Take Action》(2009)
- **ISBN**: 978-1591846444
- **URL**: TED Talk "How great leaders inspire action" (Sep 2009)
- **背景**: 觀察 Apple / Wright Bros / MLK 共同模式
- **引用**: 「People don't buy WHAT you do; they buy WHY you do it」

### Monroe's Motivated Sequence

- **作者**: Alan Monroe
- **學校**: Purdue University (1935)
- **背景**: Speech communication 教材
- **引用**: 「Attention → Need → Satisfaction → Visualization → Action — emotional + logical buildup」

### Assertion-Evidence

- **作者**: Michael Alley
- **書**: 《The Craft of Scientific Presentations》(2nd ed 2013)
- **ISBN**: 978-1441982780
- **學校**: Penn State (NSF-backed research)
- **URL**: [writing.engr.psu.edu/Slide_Style](https://writing.engr.psu.edu/slidesPSU.html)

## §5 問題分析因果

- **書**: 《Toyota Production System: Beyond Large-Scale Production》(English ed. 1988)

- **書**: 《Guide to Quality Control》(1976)
- **ISBN**: 978-9283310365

- **背景**: 名稱來自 A3 紙張大小（420×297mm）

### OODA Loop

- **作者**: John Boyd（US Air Force Colonel）

## §12 失效備援策略

每框架記錄：
1. **作者完整名** — 即使 URL 失效，作者 + 書名 仍可在 Google Books / 圖書館找到
2. **ISBN** — 國際標準書號，永久 unique identifier
3. **發表年份** — 區分版本（如 Bloom's 1956 vs 2001 修訂）
4. **一句引用** — capture 核心精神，URL / 書失效仍可保留 framework essence

每 6 個月跑一次 link audit：
```bash
# WebFetch 200 check for all URL in this file
grep -oE 'https?://[^ )]+' framework_business_sources.md | while read url; do
  curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code} $url\n" "$url"
done
```

非 200 → 標 `[URL stale; see book {ISBN}]` 並更新到新 URL（或保留書引用）。
