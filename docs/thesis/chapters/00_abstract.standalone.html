中文摘要

背景 腫瘤之亞克隆結構與演化重建傳統上以體細胞變異為依據。ONT 長讀能於同一條 read 同時取得體細胞變異、germline 單倍型與原生 5mCG 甲基化——一個自然的問題是：在 read 層級，甲基化狀態能否與多點體細胞突變「共同輔助」區分與驗證亞克隆狀況與其演化？ 本研究以 read-level 工具 ISM（read×CpG 矩陣→距離→可靠度閘控分群→單倍型/LOH 標註→normal-anchored cis-test）回答，並先以全基因組目錄界定脈絡。

規則（脈絡） 六株癌細胞株 × 三癌別共 332,705 位點之 de-confounded 目錄：可靠甲基分群僅 12,868（3.87%），且壓倒性跟隨 germline 而非 somatic；816 可測位點中僅 1 個（chr17/TBC1D16，最強 cis 候選）為乾淨 somatic cis；甲基不能做變異真偽過濾器（四道）。

主軸（climax） 然而（however），正因乾淨 somatic 訊號如此罕見，本研究於特定位點以個案（exemplars）展示其意義：ISM 能找到甲基狀態與多點體細胞突變相互佐證之情形，據以重建出與 read-level 資料一致之假想亞克隆演化結構（如 HCC1395 之 chr2 區域，體細胞變異經 ClairS 與 DeepVariant 兩呼叫器一致支持；及 chr17/TBC1D16 乾淨 cis 例證）。此為概念驗證（proof-of-concept）：甲基化扮演佐證角色而非經獨立驗證之判別器，重建之樹為與資料一致之假想結構。

結論+限制 ISM 使 read 層級整合甲基化與多點體細胞突變、於個案找出並重建亞克隆與演化結構，並以 de-confounded 目錄提供「乾淨 somatic 訊號罕見」之脈絡。主要限制/後續工作：個案尚未大規模驗證與標準化判準（future）；機制建於單樣本 HCC1395 + 單一定相流程（⭐3）；within-haplotype subclone 甲基 somatic-特異性待正確對照。

English Abstract

Background. ONT long-read captures somatic mutations, germline haplotypes and native 5mCG on the same read — can methylation states, together with multiple somatic mutations, jointly help distinguish and validate subclonal states and their evolution at read level? We address this with ISM, delimiting the genome-wide context first.

Rule (context). A de-confounded catalogue of 332,705 loci across six cell lines: reliable methylation read-clustering exists at only 12,868 (3.87%) and is overwhelmingly germline- rather than somatic-allelic; only 1/816 testable loci (chr17/TBC1D16) is a clean somatic cis signal; methylation does not discriminate true/false somatic variants (four lines).

Exemplars (climax). However, precisely because clean somatic signals are rare, case studies show where they matter: ISM finds methylation states and multiple somatic mutations that mutually corroborate, reconstructing a hypothetical subclonal evolutionary structure consistent with the read-level data (a chr2 region in HCC1395, concordantly supported by ClairS and DeepVariant; and the clean-cis exemplar chr17/TBC1D16). This is a proof-of-concept; methylation is corroborating, not an independently validated discriminator.

Conclusions. ISM enables read-level integration of methylation and multiple somatic mutations to find and—in case studies—reconstruct subclonal/evolutionary structure, contextualized by a de-confounded catalogue. Limitations: exemplars not yet validated at scale (future); single cell line + single pipeline (⭐3); within-haplotype subclone-methylation somatic-specificity awaits the correct control.
