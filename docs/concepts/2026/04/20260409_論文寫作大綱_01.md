### 投稿策略建議

**首選：Genome Research**
- 理由：(1) 有 long-read 特刊先例，審稿人熟悉 ONT 技術；(2) 接受 "honest science"——系統性探索即使結果為 negative 也有發表價值；(3) Li et al. 2025 的 long-read cancer 綜述發表於此，定位完全匹配；(4) IF 適中（7-9），與成果的 impact 相符
- 風險：F1 提升效果量極小（+0.011），若審稿人期望更大的實際效用可能被拒

**備選：Genome Biology**
- 理由：(1) IF 較高（12-14）；(2) Do et al. 2020 ASM 論文發表於此，主題直接對標；(3) 重視 computational biology 和 cancer epigenetics
- 風險：需要更強的正面結果支撐 IF 期望；self-phasing 發現若不被視為足夠 novel 可能不利

**兜底：Bioinformatics → BMC Bioinformatics**
- Bioinformatics 接受工具描述 + benchmark，適合精簡版；BMC Bioinformatics 作為最終保底

## 2. 論文定位聲明

> We present InterSubMod, a read-level methylation analysis framework for ONT long-read sequencing that characterizes epigenetic heterogeneity at somatic variant sites. Through systematic evaluation of 60+ features across 7 cancer cell-line samples (748K regions), we demonstrate that (1) allele-specific methylation is prevalent at 32-66% of somatic SNV sites but is dominated by germline cis-regulatory patterns 3-6x stronger than somatic-associated effects, (2) self-phasing circular dependency in tumor-only mode creates systematic haplotype tag artifacts affecting 38% of features — a finding with broad implications for all phasing-dependent analyses, and (3) read-level methylation provides modest but reproducible F1 improvement (+0.011, 95% CI [+0.004, +0.019]) for paired somatic calling while revealing subclonal heterogeneity markers (HPFineNGroups, TP rate 89.1% at N>=4). Our work establishes the boundaries of methylation-based variant discrimination and re-positions read-level epigenetic analysis as a characterization tool rather than a filter.

### 定位的核心邏輯

- **不是**另一個 somatic variant caller
- **不是** methylation deconvolution tool
- **是** 以 somatic SNV 為錨點的 read-level epigenetic context framework
- **獨特之處**：同時報告 positive findings（ASM 普遍性、subclone marker）和 negative findings（feature exhaustion、self-phasing artifact），提供完整的 landscape 而非 cherry-picked 結果

## 3. 故事線結構（Narrative Arc）

### Opening Hook

> Long-read sequencing uniquely enables simultaneous detection of genetic variants and epigenetic modifications on the same DNA molecule. Can this co-localized information be exploited to improve somatic variant calling and reveal tumor heterogeneity?

### Act 1 — The Promise (Introduction)

**核心訊息**：ONT long-read 的多模態能力（SNV + methylation + haplotype）尚未被系統性地用於 somatic variant verification。

段落結構：
1. **Tumor heterogeneity** 的表觀遺傳維度（背景，引用 Sharma 2010, EVOFLUx 2025）
2. **Long-read** 同時攜帶 variant + methylation 資訊的獨特優勢（引用 Li et al. 2025, Sakamoto et al. 2022）
3. **ASM at somatic sites** 的生物學動機：somatic variant 可能影響 cis-regulatory methylation（引用 Do et al. 2020）
4. **Gap statement**：目前無工具系統性地分析 read-level methylation pattern around somatic SNV sites
5. **ISM introduction**：InterSubMod 框架概述 + 論文目標聲明

### Act 2 — The Systematic Investigation (Results)

**核心訊息**：14 個月的系統性探索，涵蓋 60+ 特徵、7 樣本、748K regions，得出完整的能力邊界圖。

#### Result Section 1: ISM Framework Design
- SNV-centered 1000bp window analysis
- Read x CpG methylation matrix construction
- 6 distance metrics (NHD/L1/L2/CORR/Bernoulli/Jaccard)
- PERMANOVA significance testing + VerificationClass classification
- C++17 + OpenMP implementation, <300ms per region

#### Result Section 2: ASM Prevalence at Somatic SNV Sites (POSITIVE)
- **Finding 1**: ASM is prevalent — 32-66% of somatic sites show significant ASM (5 independent methods, 7/7 samples consistent)
- Methods concordance: Jaccard 0.78-0.83 between M2/M4/M5
- FP sites show higher ASM (46.4%) than TP (35.9%), consistent across all samples
- Interpretation: germline mQTL-driven ASM is the dominant signal

#### Result Section 3: Feature Evaluation — The Discrimination Boundary (NEGATIVE)
- **Finding 2**: No pure methylation feature achieves AUC > 0.58 for TP/FP discrimination
- Systematic evaluation: 60+ features, 7 samples, split Paired/TO
- HP-dependent features (38%) vs HP-free features (55%): HP-free all <=0.564
- Random Forest ceiling: 0.69-0.77 (HP-dependent); 0.564 (HP-free)
- **Finding 3**: Root cause — germline ASM is 3-6x stronger than somatic passenger SNV ASM
- Beyond-AUC validation: 7 methods (stratified AUC, nonlinear models, matching, residualization, interaction terms, cross-validation, literature survey) all confirm exhaustion
- Pooled OLS data snooping demonstration

#### Result Section 4: Self-Phasing Circular Dependency in Tumor-Only Mode (CRITICAL)
- **Finding 4**: Self-phasing creates systematic HP tag artifacts
- 5-step causal chain: somatic HP1:HP2 bias 17.3:1; 62% TO LOH is artifact; cross-mode r=0.001; Cohen's d=-1.20
- 38% of ISM features directly depend on HP tags — all unreliable in TO mode
- PON-only phasing correction: bias eliminated, N50 +99.7%, phased rate +23.6pp
- Implication: any phasing-dependent tool using somatic variants in the scaffold is affected

#### Result Section 5: Positive Findings — Subclonal Heterogeneity & F1 Improvement
- **Finding 5**: HPFineNGroups as subclonal heterogeneity marker
  - N>=4 with NR>=80: TP rate 89.1%, strongest signal at low AF (+50pp)
  - 7/7 sample consistency; residualized AUC=0.617
  - Not usable as a filter (AUC<0.85), but captures real subclonal structure
- **Finding 6**: Modest paired F1 improvement
  - +0.0112, 95% CI [+0.0044, +0.0188], McNemar p=1.44e-51
  - HCC1395 5kHz: F1 0.8443 -> 0.8532
  - TO mode: negative or negligible effect (-0.0206)
  - Honest caveat: discovery holdout CI contains zero; H2009 negative direction

### Act 3 — The Pivot & Value (Discussion)

**核心訊息**：ISM 的價值在 characterization 而非 filtering；negative findings 本身具有重要學術意義。

段落結構：
1. **Why methylation cannot filter variants** — germline ASM fundamentally indistinguishable from somatic in the absence of normal reference; this is a biological boundary, not a technical limitation
2. **Self-phasing implications for the field** — all tools that include somatic variants in phasing scaffold are affected; recommendation for PON-only phasing in TO mode
3. **ISM as characterization tool** — ASM quantification, subclonal structure (HPFineNGroups), VerificationClass as annotation rather than hard filter
4. **Comparison with related work** — NanoMethPhase (genome-wide ASM), PRISM (bulk deconvolution), MethylBERT (supervised ML), EVOFLUx (fCpG clonal tracking); ISM fills the read-level per-SNV niche
5. **Future directions** — (a) normal methylation reference to separate germline from somatic ASM; (b) copy-number/purity-aware read-label modeling; (c) gene-level evidence integration for biallelic inactivation; (d) CpG functional stratification
6. **Limitations** — cell-line benchmarks only (high purity 80-95%); single PON sample validated; F1 effect size small; low-purity clinical samples untested

## 9. 審稿人可能質疑 & 預備回應

預先列出最可能的審稿意見及回應策略，寫作時主動防禦。

### Q1: "F1 improvement is too small (+0.011) to be practically useful."

**回應策略**：
- 明確承認 effect size 小，定位為 proof-of-concept
- 強調論文主要貢獻不在 F1 而在 (a) ASM landscape characterization, (b) self-phasing discovery, (c) exhaustion boundary delineation
- 類比：許多高影響力方法論文的實際改善幅度也不大，但建立的 framework 和 findings 有持久價值
- 主文中主動以 "modest but reproducible" 描述

### Q2: "All benchmarks are on high-purity cell lines. Does this generalize to clinical samples?"

**回應策略**：
- Limitations section 主動承認
- 論述高純度是最佳條件——若高純度下都無法區分 TP/FP，低純度更不可能（exhaustion 結論更強）
- 反面論述：HPFineNGroups 在低純度下可能反而更有價值（稀釋使 subclone 更明顯）
- 列為 Future Work 重點

### Q3: "Have you tried [deep learning / transformer / graph neural network]?"

**回應策略**：
- 引用 Beyond-AUC 7 方法驗證（Fig.5A），包含 nonlinear models（Random Forest）
- 指出 feature space exhaustion 是 data-level boundary，非 model-level — 更複雜的模型不會產生更好的輸入
- 引用 MethylBERT (2025): 即使 supervised DL 在 bulk deconvolution 有效，read-level per-SNV 的信噪比根本不同
- Supplementary S7 中展示 RF ceiling

### Q4: "Self-phasing finding is not new — it's obvious that including somatic variants in phasing would cause bias."

**回應策略**：
- "Obvious in hindsight" ≠ "previously quantified and published"
- 提供精確量化（17.3:1 bias, 62% LOH artifact, -1.20 Cohen's d）— 這些數字是原創的
- 引用 LongPhase paper 和 Sakamoto et al. — 均未提及此問題
- 展示 7/7 跨樣本一致性，證明非 artifact 而是 systematic

### Q5: "Only 7 samples — statistical power is limited."

**回應策略**：
- 每個樣本有數萬至十萬 regions（748K total），統計功率來自 region 數而非 sample 數
- 7 samples 的跨樣本一致性（HPFineNGroups 7/7, self-phasing 7/7）已提供 consistency evidence
- 列為 limitation 但指出 SEQC2 是目前最完整的 ONT cancer cell-line benchmark

### Q6: "The negative findings are not surprising — germline ASM dominating is expected."

**回應策略**：
- 引用 Do et al. 2020：ASM 在 normal 已普遍，cancer 增加但機制相同
- 重點在 quantification：我們第一次在 read-level 精確量化了 germline ASM 對 TP/FP discrimination 的 ceiling effect
- 比較：BSseq/array-based ASM studies 無法提供 read-level 解析度
- 45+ 篇文獻 survey 支持 exhaustion 非孤立結論
