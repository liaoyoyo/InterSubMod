## Slide 4: 目標說明

目標說明

Concept Explanation

建立一套針對 腫瘤/正常 Oxford Nanopore 資料的高效分析系統
整合 體細胞變異 (somatic variants) 與 DNA 甲基化 (single-molecule methylation)，對每一個 somatic SNV 周邊區域，建構以讀段為單位的 CpG 甲基化距離矩陣，並進行讀序分群與演化樹推斷，評估群集與單倍型 (haplotype)、等位基因 (REF/ALT)、腫瘤/正常標籤之關聯性，達到：
輔助 somatic VCF 真實性驗證與偽陽性過濾
解析等位特異性甲基化 (ASM)
腫瘤次克隆結構 (subclones)
以 C++ 多執行緒架構下，提供可重現、可擴充的整合分析程式

We aim to develop a high-performance analysis framework for paired tumor/normal Oxford Nanopore sequencing data. The system integrates somatic variants and single-molecule DNA methylation to construct, for each somatic SNV, a read-level CpG methylation distance matrix within the local genomic region. Based on this representation, we perform read clustering and phylogenetic inference, and evaluate the association between clusters and haplotypes, allelic status (REF/ALT), as well as tumor/normal labels, in order to:
support somatic VCF validation and false-positive variant filtering,
resolve allele-specific methylation (ASM), and
characterize tumor subclonal (subclone) architecture.
The framework is implemented in C++ with a multi-threaded design, providing a reproducible and extensible integrated analysis tool.
